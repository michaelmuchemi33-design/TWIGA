2. What TWIGA is

TWIGA's primary product is an Android telephone application that acts as a phone-side telephony bridge for a business using its existing handset and SIM, alongside an online business workspace and administration service. Available features depend on Android permissions, default-dialler status, handset manufacturer, Android version, carrier/network support, internet connectivity, provider configuration and subscription status.

3. SIM and AI call limitations

Android does not grant ordinary third-party applications unrestricted access to cellular call audio. Selecting TWIGA as the default phone app does not by itself grant a universal ability to stream SIM-call audio to an AI provider. Some carriers, plans, handsets or call types may not support this. TWIGA will not claim that AI has answered or spoken on a SIM call unless the relevant call path has been successfully established. In-app internet voice tests are not proof that SIM calls are bridged.
