# TWIGA website design instructions

Use the Composio skills **Landing Page Design** and **No AI Design Slop** for every marketing or UI change. Preserve the existing product direction and improve it deliberately rather than replacing it with a generic template.

## TWIGA identity
- Use the supplied giraffe emblem as the source of truth. Do not redraw, simplify, crop off, recolour, or substitute it with a generic lettermark.
- Brand palette: orange #F47721, black #111111, white #FFFFFF, quiet neutral #F6F6F5.
- White is the dominant surface, black carries typography and structure, orange is a deliberate accent.
- Keep the Kenyan small-business context specific and plain spoken.

## Product truth
TWIGA is an AI receptionist for Kenyan businesses. It answers calls, handles configured FAQs, captures enquiries, can book appointments against configured availability, and gives the business team call activity and follow-up context. Do not imply that every integration or call-routing path is universally enabled; describe setup dependencies accurately.
- Main conversion: start a 7-day trial at https://app.twiga.co.ke/start.
- Android download: https://github.com/michaelmuchemi33-design/twiga-android-app/releases/download/latest-apk/twiga.apk
- Current stable APK asset name: twiga.apk; release metadata: version.json.

## Design quality gate
- No invented customer counts, testimonials, reviews, business logos, or outcome statistics.
- Avoid generic gradients, excessive floating cards, glassmorphism, random blobs, pointless motion, and stock AI dashboard mockups.
- Use real product UI and relevant local business photography where it helps explain the service.
- Keep interactions accessible, keyboard reachable, mobile-first and respectful of prefers-reduced-motion.
- Cursor effects must be restrained, pointer-only, and disabled for touch/reduced-motion.
- Keep typography, spacing, radii, and transitions consistent with the current site; do not blindly add new frameworks.
