GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Here's what most businesses want to know before getting started.

Do I need a new phone number?+

No new SIM is required for the phone-side bridge. TWIGA is designed around your existing Android handset and SIM. AI voice joining a normal cellular call depends on a supported carrier conference-call or approved network bridge; not every line or phone supports it.

Can TWIGA actually book appointments?+

Yes, when connected to your chosen calendar and configured with your services, staff availability and booking rules. It can check open times and confirm the appointment flow you approve.

Can it understand Kenyan customers?+

TWIGA is designed for Kenyan businesses and can be configured for the language and style your customers use, including English and Kiswahili. Voice and language quality depend on the selected assistant configuration.

What if a customer needs a real person?+

TWIGA can capture the request and route or flag it for your team, based on the hand-off rules configured for your business.

How do payments work?+

Paid access is KSh 375 for 7 days, or KSh 1,500 for four weeks (28 days). Payments are one-off, with no automatic renewal. There are 3 grace days after the paid-through date; after that TWIGA cloud workspace features pause until payment is confirmed. Your normal phone, calls and personal WhatsApp continue working.
