Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Who operates TWIGA?
TWIGA is operated by Michael Muchemi. For privacy and support enquiries, contact support@twiga.co.ke. TWIGA does not publish a walk-in office address.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the TWIGA Android telephone application, business workspace, online administration panel, website, AI calling features, integrations and support services (“TWIGA” or “Services”). It explains what information is processed, why it is used, who may receive it and the choices available to account owners and people who contact a TWIGA-enabled business.

2. Information processed

Account and business details: account email, user ID, business name, business type, team membership, business profile, working hours, greetings, services, products, prices, stock notes, policies and AI instructions supplied by the business.

Phone and call information: phone numbers, call direction, call state, time, duration, device and carrier diagnostics, call routing commands, and call outcomes. Depending on the selected provider and enabled settings, TWIGA may also process call audio, transcripts, summaries and structured information extracted from conversations.

Business records: customer names and phone numbers, enquiries, orders, order items and totals, appointment requests, follow-up tasks, notes and call history.

Device information: Android version, app version, device pairing token, permissions, diagnostic events and connectivity information needed to keep the phone bridge operating.

Connected accounts and payments: data from Google Calendar, Contacts, Sheets or Drive only when the business owner connects and authorises the relevant integration; subscription status, payment references and transaction confirmations from payment providers. TWIGA does not ask for or store mobile-money PINs or one-time passcodes.

3. How TWIGA uses information

Information is used to operate the Android phone bridge; detect and manage calls; route calls according to the business's settings; connect supported AI voice sessions; identify callers where available; answer questions using business-approved knowledge; capture enquiries and order or appointment requests; create follow-up tasks; synchronise authorised business records; provide support; secure accounts and devices; administer subscriptions; and comply with legal obligations.

4. The Android telephone bridge and SIM calls

The TWIGA Android app is the phone-side bridge and is designed to work with the handset and SIM already used by the business. It may use Android's supported dialler, Telecom, call-state and call-control interfaces after the user grants permissions and selects TWIGA as the default phone app where required. Android and mobile-network restrictions differ by device, OS version and carrier. Ordinary third-party Android apps are not universally permitted to capture the protected audio stream of a cellular SIM call. Where AI participation in a SIM call is enabled, it requires a supported call-conference or carrier/network bridge path; otherwise TWIGA may manage call events and controls without streaming the cellular audio to AI. TWIGA must not imply that every SIM, carrier or handset supports every AI call feature.

5. AI, voice and telephony providers

When the business selects Retell AI, Vapi or another supported provider, the information necessary to operate that call may be sent to that provider and its configured speech, voice, model, telephony or hosting subcontractors. The selected provider processes the information under its own terms and privacy documentation. Provider choice is controlled by the business or TWIGA administrator according to the account configuration. TWIGA does not sell personal information.

6. Recording, transcription and caller notice

Recording and transcription are optional features and may be controlled by the business administrator and provider capability. When enabled, the business is responsible for giving callers clear notice and obtaining consent or other lawful authority required under applicable law. A call may still generate basic technical metadata even when audio recording is off. Businesses should disable recording when it is not needed and configure retention settings before enabling it.

7. Business separation and access

TWIGA is designed to keep each business's workspace, customers, orders, appointments, calls, prompts and provider configuration separated by business account. Owners should invite only authorised staff and remove access when it is no longer required. TWIGA personnel may access relevant information only where needed for support, security, maintenance or legal compliance, subject to access controls.

8. Connected services and international processing

If an owner connects Google or another external service, TWIGA accesses only the scopes approved in that connection and uses the data to provide the requested integration. Cloud, AI, telephony and storage providers may process information outside Kenya. TWIGA should use appropriate contractual and security safeguards for international transfers as required by applicable law. Each external provider's own privacy terms also apply.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Services, maintain account and transaction records, support business operations, protect the platform, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Call audio, transcripts and summaries may have separate provider and storage retention periods; the period displayed in settings must reflect the actual provider configuration. Deleting information in TWIGA may not automatically delete copies already held by a voice provider, Google, an export, a device backup or another connected service. Account closure and deletion requests can be made at support@twiga.co.ke.

10. Security

TWIGA uses authentication, tenant-scoped access controls, encrypted network connections and server-side storage for provider credentials where supported. API keys must not be embedded in the Android package or public website code. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect devices and passwords, use trusted devices, and report suspected account compromise promptly.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable Kenyan law, individuals may request access to or correction of personal information, ask for deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, and raise a complaint with the relevant authority. Contact support@twiga.co.ke. A business using TWIGA is generally responsible for responding to its customers' privacy requests and providing the notices required for its own customer interactions.

12. Children and sensitive information

TWIGA is a business service, not a service intended for children to create accounts independently. Businesses should not collect sensitive personal information through the AI receptionist unless they have a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards. Never disclose passwords, PINs, OTPs, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information to an AI assistant.

13. Cookies, changes and contact

The website and workspace may use essential cookies or similar technologies for login, security and operation. We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change and will publish the revised effective date. For privacy or support matters, contact support@twiga.co.ke. Operator: Michael Muchemi. No physical office address is published for this online service.
